# Executive Summary: Pet Wellness Tracking Market Analysis

**Quick Reference — Key Findings & Recommendations**

---

## The Opportunity

**79% of Americans** want app-based pet health monitoring, yet no dominant app provides a truly **comprehensive pet wellness platform**. The market is fragmented across 10+ competing apps, each solving only 1-2 aspects of pet care.

## The Gap

**No existing app combines:**
- ✅ Comprehensive health tracking (feeding, vaccines, medications, weight, behavior, mental wellness)
- ✅ AI-powered predictive health insights
- ✅ Family/caregiver collaboration
- ✅ Emotional/gamified engagement
- ✅ Beautiful, intuitive design
- ✅ Smart home/IoT integration

## The Solution

Build **PawTable** — a unified, emotionally engaging pet wellness companion for all pet types, with AI insights, family sharing, and delightful design.

## Monetization Strategy

### Recommended Model: **Freemium with Tiered Subscription**

| Tier | Price | Target |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Free** | $0 | User acquisition, habit formation |
| **Monthly** | $6.99/mo | Casual users, short-term commitments |
| **Annual** | $49.99/yr (~$4.17/mo) | **Primary conversion target** (40% discount drives commitment) |
| **Family** | $9.99/mo or $79.99/yr | Multi-pet households (60% of dog owners have 2+ pets) |

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Freemium conversion rate | 5-7% |
| Annual plan share | 75-80% |
| Day 30 paid retention | 70-75% |
| LTV:CAC ratio | 15-20x |

## Go-to-Market Strategy

1. **Freemium model** — Free basic tracking, $6.99/month premium (see above)
2. **Cross-platform** — iOS + Android (Flutter/React Native)
3. **Content-driven growth** — Pet health blog, breed guides, social media
4. **Community-first** — Breed groups, influencer partnerships, vet referrals
5. **Phased rollout** — Core → AI → Integrations → Ecosystem

## UI/UX Key Principles

- **Warm, inviting design** — Soft pastels, pet illustrations, emotional connection
- **One-thumb navigation** — Bottom nav, quick-log buttons, swipe actions
- **Gamification** — Streaks, milestones, celebration animations
- **Family-first** — Multi-user from day one
- **Dark mode** — Essential for night-time emergencies
- **Offline support** — Critical for emergency use
