## Recommended Name: PAW TABLE

### Rationale

**PawTable** uniquely captures the emotional core of pet ownership while being distinctive, brandable, and scalable.

The name works on multiple levels:
1. **Literal:** A table for pets — where you share meals together
2. **Metaphorical:** "At the table" — your pet is family, included in everything
3. **Functional:** A "table" of data — organized health records, schedules, insights

## Brand Positioning

### Taglines
1. **"Your Pet, At Your Table"** — *Emotional, family-focused*
2. **"Wellness for the Whole Family"** — *Inclusive, warm*
3. **"Every Pet Deserves a Seat"** — *Advocacy, caring*
4. **"Track. Care. Celebrate."** — *Action-oriented, joyful*

### Brand Voice
- **Warm and caring** — like a trusted friend who loves animals
- **Knowledgeable but approachable** — expert without being clinical
- **Joyful and encouraging** — celebrates milestones, doesn't shame
- **Inclusive** — works for all pet types, all experience levels

### Brand Personality (Brand Archetype)
**The Caregiver + The Jester** — nurturing and protective, but also playful and fun. Pet care shouldn't feel like a chore; it should feel like an act of love that also has moments of joy and humor.

## Naming Framework Used

This analysis followed established naming principles:

1. **Relevance** — The name connects to the pet/wellness category
2. **Differentiation** — The name stands out from competitors
3. **Memorability** — The name is easy to remember and spell
4. **Versatility** — The name works across platforms, regions, languages
5. **Scalability** — The name doesn't limit future growth
6. **Emotional appeal** — The name evokes positive feelings
7. **Legal viability** — The name can be trademarked and protected
